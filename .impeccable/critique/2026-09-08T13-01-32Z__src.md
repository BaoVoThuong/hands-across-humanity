#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Documentary imagery and a warm, restrained action palette already give the site a credible community character. The donation experience does not yet earn the premium transparency position: it changes an amount and cadence, but not a verified, concrete outcome. The main content describes Houston-facing donated goods, volunteer preparation, and local partners, whereas `PRODUCT.md` describes international water, housing, and infrastructure projects. Those cannot both be the donor story.

**Deterministic scan:** The focused donation scan returned `[]`. A repo-wide scan returned advisory design-system drift warnings, principally use of Montserrat and colour literals that do not match `DESIGN.md` (which specifies Newsreader/Public Sans and a different palette). This is a system-maintenance issue, not the primary donation-conversion blocker.

#### Overall Impression

The site now makes donating easy to discover, but it asks for money before it has established a specific, verifiable reason to give. The best next move is not more generic Donate buttons. It is one coherent promise: which community is being served, what cash enables beyond in-kind goods, what a chosen gift makes possible, and how the donor is protected and updated.

#### What is working

- Persistent header Donate access is useful without being visually dominant.
- The `$25–$500`, custom amount, and one-time/monthly controls are legible and work on mobile without horizontal overflow.
- Authentic field photos create a strong proof base for a local-community story.
- The About page has an effective full donation card; it should become the detailed commitment pattern rather than be cloned everywhere.

#### Priority issues

- **[P0] Establish a single factual donor narrative before expanding copy.** `PRODUCT.md` promises international projects, water, housing, and infrastructure; the public site shows local donated goods, schools, health/hygiene, volunteer work, and Houston-facing activities. Confirm the truth, geographic scope, tax/receipt status, fees, and cash-allocation policy. Do not invent per-dollar outcomes until verified.

- **[P0] A Donate action does not yet lead to a donor-ready transaction.** Without `PUBLIC_DONATION_URL`, the panel advertises a secure flow then resolves to a mailto link, labelled “Connect about giving.” Configure a real processor and name it, or deliberately call the feature an inquiry/pledge flow until it exists. A donor needs a clear receipt, privacy, and next-step explanation at commitment time.

- **[P1] Replace repetition with contextual asks.** Every editorial page gets the same `DonationPrompt`, then another “Donate now” in the next-chapter block. This clusters asks at the bottom, after the reader’s moment of interest, and makes the prompt generic. Keep the header and footer utility links, but use one contextual ask per page after that page’s strongest proof: Mission → prepare an urgent handoff; Work → support a verified focus area; Events → equip the next distribution; Vision → become a monthly partner. Remove the duplicate chapter Donate link where that contextual ask exists.

- **[P1] Make amount selection mean something, or stop implying it does.** The summary changes only `$50` and cadence. “See how far your gift goes,” “direct allocation,” and “direct impact” promise a calculation that is not shown. Use verified outcome units for each amount (with methodology), or use modest copy such as “Choose a gift for the general support fund.”

- **[P1] Give every page one distinct job.** About should establish the community and proof; Mission should explain urgency and who is served; Work should show programmes / costs; Events should demonstrate participation and present an immediate local need; Vision should make the case for recurring support; Donate should complete the transaction and reassure. “Practical support moving” currently repeats across all of them, reducing recall instead of reinforcing it.

- **[P2] Convert existing high-intent moments.** The FAQ contains “Giving with confidence” and “Want to take part?” but sends users only to Work. Add a second, honest gift path there, and surface FAQs about receipts, allocation, privacy, and contact rather than hiding trust information at the final handoff.

- **[P2] Optimise mobile commitment.** On a phone the donor selects cadence and amount before seeing the final CTA. Keep a compact sticky “Give $50 once” action or shorten the summary so the selected amount, benefit, and action can be reviewed together.

- **[P2] Reconcile the design source of truth.** `DESIGN.md` is stale relative to the implementation; its typography/palette guidance conflicts with current components. Refresh the sidecar before another visual sweep, otherwise future UI work will keep reintroducing drift.

#### Recommended donation journey

1. **Need:** a page-specific fact or current priority.
2. **Proof:** an authentic photograph, short field detail, partner / place, and only verified metrics.
3. **Ask:** one clear context-specific action, e.g. “Support school-ready kits” rather than generic “Donate now.”
4. **Reassurance:** payment platform, receipt / tax status where applicable, privacy, allocation method, and a human contact.
5. **Commitment:** the full donation panel only on `/donate/` and once on About after field proof; other pages link into a preselected theme/cadence.

#### Persona red flags

- **First-time mobile donor:** sees a polished amount selector but reaches an ambiguous email handoff, which creates a trust drop at the decisive moment.
- **Local community member:** sees volunteer/goods stories but cannot tell why cash is needed in addition to donated goods.
- **Corporate or foundation partner:** cannot reconcile public local-programme evidence with the international-project claims in the product document, and has no audit, reporting, or allocation evidence.

#### Minor observations

- Five presets plus a custom amount is more choice than the compact module needs; `$25 / $50 / $100 / Other` is easier to scan until genuine tiers exist.
- “Direct allocation engine” reads like fintech jargon, not a community nonprofit. Replace it with a plain factual label such as “Choose your gift” or a verified campaign name.
- The active amount’s midnight fill is visually attractive but the information tone should be carried by the selected state, not by a pseudo-financial dashboard aesthetic.
- The existing authentic image library is more persuasive than decorative icons; use a contextual image/caption before asks rather than adding more UI chrome.
