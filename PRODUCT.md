# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7, Tailwind CSS v4, Vanilla CSS

## Users

Donors, corporate partners, and humanitarian foundation decision-makers seeking transparent, high-impact community initiatives.

## Product Purpose

Hands Across Humanity connects international donors and partners directly with sustainable, ground-level humanitarian projects in clean water access, education infrastructure, community housing, and local resilience.

## Positioning

A premium, high-trust commercial humanitarian platform that pairs radical field transparency and real impact metrics with effortless partner engagement and clear donor pathways.

## Operating Context

Client-facing web presentation and donor engagement platform. Used by foundations, grant evaluators, and major donors during partnership discussions, campaign outreach, and funding presentations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- High-impact hero layout with strategic messaging and call-to-actions
- Comprehensive impact statistics, active program showcases, field project stories, trust indicators, and partner testimonials
- Responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports with fluid layout transitions
- Astro static architecture with optimal performance and clean CSS styling

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hands Across Humanity
- Tone: Modern, Premium, Professional, Commercial, Clean, High-Trust
- Visual Identity: Elevated editorial and modern non-profit aesthetics with crisp visual hierarchy

## Evidence on Hand

- High-resolution authentic field photos in `src/assets/images/`
- Established page routes: Home (`/`), Mission (`/mission/`), What We Do (`/what-we-do/`), Vision (`/vision/`), Who We Are (`/who-we-are/`)

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity First**: Instant visual comprehension of impact, mission, and how support transforms communities.
2. **Commercial Authority**: Exclude generic template looks; project the authority of a world-class humanitarian foundation.
3. **Structured Hierarchy**: Seamless visual flow from hero hooks to metrics, field initiatives, proof points, and converting CTAs.
4. **Purposeful Polish**: Micro-interactions, dynamic cards, elegant color palette, crisp typography hierarchy, and generous whitespace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast compliance, semantic HTML layout, keyboard accessibility, and descriptive text alternatives for all visuals.
